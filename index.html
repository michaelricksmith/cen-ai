State 06 · Safety, built in while it trains
Safety messages

Safety guidance is part of Blackwell’s training material itself, so expected behavior is learned during training, not patched on afterward.

Eight model principles

Blackwell is built around eight principles covering truth, harm, fairness, and accountability, the standards its answers are measured against.

File handling

Blackwell is being taught to work with documents: read a file, summarize it, and work on its contents, with the same safety principles applied to what it reads and writes.

State 09 · The founder — a note in the log

I am Michael Smith. I taught myself this work, and I have ten years of building behind me. I did not wait for a lab to hire me or a team to hand me the hard parts. I built the lab.
