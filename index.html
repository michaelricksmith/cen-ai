Honest eval gates at each step: a larger model is only trained when the smaller one finishes and its evaluations say the system is ready. Results are reported as measured, including where they fall short.

State 06 · Safety, built in while it trains
Safety messages

Safety guidance is part of the training material itself, so expected behavior is learned during training, not patched on afterward.

Eight model principles

The model is built around eight principles covering truth, harm, fairness, and accountability, the standards its answers are measured against.

File handling

MDean is being taught to work with documents: read a file, summarize it, and work on its contents, with the same safety principles applied to what it reads and writes.

State 09 · The founder — a note in the log

I am Michael Smith. I taught myself this work, and I have ten years of building behind me. I did not wait for a lab to hire me or a team to hand me the hard parts. I built the lab.

Nobody handed me an education in this either. I learned it myself over ten years, through books, Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube, lesson by lesson, usually after work. School came later: I am a working adult now in my third year of a BS in Business Information Systems, with a career across several levels of technology behind me. I have also done contract work evaluating and training frontier AI models, the kind of work where you learn exactly how the best models are judged, and where they fall short.

Centralia is mine, built solo, from scratch: the custom tokenizer, the 19.5 billion token data diet, the training harness I wrote by hand, and MDean, the 1 billion parameter model that is 68.7 percent trained right now. Nobody handed me a stack. I am the stack.

I measure everything and I post the numbers as they are, good or bad. That is the whole pitch: I ship, I measure honestly, and I keep going. What comes next is simple. Finish MDean. Run the eval gates and publish what they say. Add the safety work and file handling. Then scale to the 3B.

— MICHAEL SMITH, FOUNDER, CENTRALIA COGNITION LABS
